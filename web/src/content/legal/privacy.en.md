# PRIVACY POLICY — English

**Version 1.0 — Draft**

## 1. Introduction

**LearnChinese Today** is a Mandarin-learning platform operated by **ITCS WEBPLACE LTDA**, Brazilian company registration **65.998.990/0001-44**, based in São Paulo, Brazil (“Webplace”). This Policy covers **https://learnchinese.today**, the **LearnChinese Today** iOS app and its integrated features, including **Hanzi Memorize** (memorization and the app’s own widget) and **Phrase Games**. **Hanzi Memorize is not a separate app or service.** Webplace is the controller of personal data processed under its responsibility. Contact: **email@learnchinese.today**.

## 2. Principles and service characteristics

We seek to process only data appropriate for educational, security and service-improvement purposes. The platform is currently free, displays no ads, does not sell personal data, does not request IDFA, and does not require precise GPS, camera, microphone, contacts or photos for its educational functions. Analytics and diagnostic tools are described below; no advertising does not mean no technical data processing.

## 3. Categories of data

Depending on use, we process: **(a)** email, name or nickname, internal account ID and sign-in provider IDs; **(b)** protected credentials, tokens and session records; **(c)** exercises, rounds, correct answers, scores, HSK levels, study history and preferences; **(d)** visited pages, usage events, campaign sources, language, browser, operating system, technical or pseudonymous identifiers and crash data; **(e)** IP addresses and connection logs when needed for operations and security. Registration currently does not request date of birth. Users may use an Apple private relay email address.

## 4. Purposes of processing

We use data to provide the website and app, authenticate accounts, synchronize progress, offer exercises and memorization features, respond to support requests, secure infrastructure, investigate failures, understand usage and improve learning, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

## 5. Legal bases

Depending on the purpose and applicable law, processing may rely on **performance of a contract** (account, authentication and progress), properly assessed **legitimate interests** (security, diagnostics and proportionate improvements), **legal obligations**, or **consent** where required, particularly for non-essential analytics technologies. Relevant GDPR legal bases and requirements also apply where applicable. Acceptance of the Terms does not replace specific consent when the law requires it.

## 6. Third-party sign-in

We may offer sign-in through **Apple, Google, Alipay** or email, subject to availability. We receive data needed to link and authenticate accounts according to the method used. These providers may also independently process data under their own policies. Linking accounts may require email confirmation or another security check.

## 7. AI learning tutor

The website tutor processes questions on Webplace-managed infrastructure using a locally hosted model, **without forwarding them to public commercial AI services**. The application does not keep a permanent conversation history in its database; messages remain temporarily in browser memory during the session. We do not automatically add the user’s name, email or account ID to the text sent to the model. However, personal information voluntarily typed by the user may be included. **Do not enter passwords, sensitive data or confidential information.** Technical connection logs may be retained for security and operations, not to archive conversations.

## 8. Google Analytics on the website

The website uses **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to evaluate visits, navigation and educational interactions such as completed rounds, vocabulary lookups and opening the tutor, as well as campaign information. It may process cookies, pseudonymous identifiers and technical data; **pseudonymization is not anonymization**. Under the reported implementation, the analytics script does not load before acceptance through the banner. Declining or not responding does not prevent access to basic website features. We do not use this integration to display ads.

## 9. Firebase in the app

Versions of the iOS app may use **Firebase Analytics** for usage events and **Firebase Crashlytics** for crash diagnostics. Depending on the released version’s configuration, these tools may process technical identifiers, a pseudonymous account ID, exercise events, device characteristics and error reports. Such data may be personal data. The integration was reported as implemented but not yet released in the version reviewed; App Store privacy disclosures must match the version actually distributed. Under the reported configuration, the app does not request IDFA or perform advertising tracking across other companies’ services.

## 10. Hanzi Memorize and the integrated widget

**Hanzi Memorize is a feature of the LearnChinese Today app**, like **Phrase Games**; it is not a separate app. The widget provided by the same app may display characters on the iPhone Home or Lock Screen. It uses local storage shared by the app and its extension for session status and preferences such as rotation interval, translation and Pinyin. According to the technical documentation, the widget does not store email or individual telemetry history in that shared space.

## 11. Service providers and sharing

We may share data necessary to provide the service with **Hetzner** (hosting), **Apple** (sign-in and App Store), **Google** (sign-in, GA4 and Firebase tools), **Alipay/Ant Group** (sign-in, when available) and **Resend** (transactional emails). Providers may act as processors or independent controllers depending on the activity. Sharing is limited to the purposes described and is not a sale of personal data.

## 12. International transfers

The primary infrastructure and database are reported to be hosted in **Germany**, with contingency backups in **Brazil**. Authentication, analytics, diagnostics and email services may involve other countries, including the United States and other locations depending on the provider. Webplace will observe legally required mechanisms and safeguards for international transfers under Brazil’s LGPD and, where applicable, the GDPR. A server’s location alone does not replace legal assessment of the transfer.

## 13. Retention and backups

Account and progress data are kept as needed to provide features, subject to lawful retention exceptions. Under the reported configuration, technical access logs may remain for up to **14 days**, and physical database backups rotate within **30 days**. Provider-held data may have separate retention periods. Account deletion does not instantly erase existing backup copies; if a backup is restored, prior deletion requests must remain effective.

## 14. Account deletion and statistics

Users may delete their account through available in-app controls or contact **email@learnchinese.today**. The reported process removes registration data, linked sign-in methods, sessions, progress and account-related rounds from the operational database. Historical events may be retained for statistical or operational purposes where lawful. **Removing `user_id` alone does not ensure anonymization:** records containing `anon_id`, `round_id`, dates, user-agent or other attributes may still be personal data if identification remains reasonably possible. Only effectively anonymized statistics fall outside personal-data rules as provided by law. Mandatory retention and backups remain subject to this Policy.

## 15. Data subject rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion or restriction where legally available, portability where applicable, sharing information, objection, withdrawal of consent and review of automated decisions when relevant. Requests can be sent to **email@learnchinese.today**. We may require proportionate identity verification. There is currently no automatic data-export button; copy or portability requests may be sent to support.

## 16. Cookies and controls

The website may use technologies needed for sessions, authentication, preferences and security. Non-essential analytics cookies, including GA4 cookies, require prior authorization where the law requires it. Declining does not prevent basic use. The iOS app may use SDK identifiers rather than browser cookies; their purposes and relevant controls are addressed in the sections above.

## 17. Security

We apply proportionate technical and organizational measures to protect accounts, credentials, sessions and infrastructure against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No internet-connected service is completely immune to incidents. We will notify authorities and individuals when legally required.

## 18. Minimum age and teenagers

The service is intended for people aged **16 or older**. Registration currently does not collect dates of birth. If we learn that an account does not meet the minimum age, we may restrict or delete it in accordance with legal requirements. The age limit does not remove legal protections applicable to teenagers.

## 19. International users and updates

The service may be accessed internationally. We respect mandatory data-protection rules applicable to users as relevant. This Policy may change to reflect technical, operational or legal developments. The current version will be published at **https://learnchinese.today/privacy**, with additional notice where required. Translations are provided for accessibility and do not limit mandatory statutory rights.

## 20. Contact
